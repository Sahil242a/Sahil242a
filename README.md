## hey, I'm Sahil 👋

I build AI systems that work on messy, real-world input — blurry number plates, handwritten answer sheets, crowded clinic waiting rooms — and wrap them in apps people can actually use. I like tracing a problem end to end: the model, the API, the UI, and the honest caveats.

**Currently:** going deeper into computer vision, RAG and production-minded AI applications.

**Looking for:** opportunities in **AI/ML engineering, full-stack development or software engineering**. [Say hello](mailto:gsahil1234567@gmail.com) — I'd love to hear what your team is building.
